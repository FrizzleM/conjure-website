The code

Say it. Paste it.
Scan it.

Every transfer comes down to one code, whatever route it took. Codes are forgiving: hyphens and capitals don't matter, and an O typed for a 0 still works. A typo is caught on your device before anything is downloaded.

Anyone who has the code can get the file, so share it the way you'd share the file itself.

CJ1042004G024S2TXV1EHJQ4V3FDWPQAW3JDXQQ9KJJ

A real code, for a live transfer. It stops working when the transfer ends.

Everything else

Small app.
Careful details.

Private when you ask.

Turn on Private and Conjure encrypts the file on your device before it leaves. The service that holds it sees scrambled bytes and a meaningless name. The key travels inside the code.

You send quarterly-report.pdf
The host sees x7Qf-9aKe2m0Lr5VzT8w
They get quarterly-report.pdf

Three routes, chosen for you.

Direct over the internet, across your local network, or stored for four days for someone who isn't online yet.

Checked on arrival.

Downloads are verified with SHA‑256 before they're saved. A broken copy never lands in your folder.

Several files, one code.

Pick a handful and they travel together. The recipient never has to unzip anything.

No account.

Nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to. Open the app and send.

At home in the terminal.
